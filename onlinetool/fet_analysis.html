1. 典型背栅 PbS CQD FET 结构

1. Typical Back-Gated PbS CQD FET

迁移率、阈值电压、开关比都依赖几何参数。没有 W、L 和氧化层厚度，迁移率不应给出具体数值。

Mobility, threshold voltage, and on/off ratio all depend on geometry. Without W, L, and oxide thickness, a numeric mobility is not defensible.

顶接触/底栅结构中，n++ Si 作为栅极，SiO₂ 作为栅介质，PbS CQD 薄膜位于中间作为沟道，Source/Drain 金属电极在 PbS 薄膜上方接触沟道。x 方向从源极指向漏极，y 方向为垂直薄膜方向。
In the top-contact/bottom-gate geometry, n++ Si is the gate, SiO₂ is the gate dielectric, the PbS CQD film is the channel in the middle, and the Source/Drain metals contact the PbS film from the top. The x-axis points from source to drain, and the y-axis is vertical to the film.

2. FET 分析的核心推理链

2. Core Reasoning Chain for FET Analysis

FET 分析不是先算迁移率，而是逐步回答：电流从哪里来、是否受栅压控制、受什么因素限制。

FET analysis should not start from mobility. It should first identify where the current comes from, whether it is gate-controlled, and what limits it.

1. 栅极是否真正控制沟道？

1. Is the channel gate-controlled?

IG ≪ ID

先排除栅漏电和绝缘层击穿，再判断 ID 是否随 VG 系统变化。

Exclude gate leakage and dielectric breakdown first, then check whether ID changes systematically with VG.

2. 电流由接触还是沟道限制？

2. Contact-limited or channel-limited?

ID-VD

低压线性更接近欧姆接触；强非线性、整流或注入门槛常提示接触势垒。

A linear low-bias region suggests ohmic-like contact; strong nonlinearity, rectification, or injection threshold suggests contact barriers.

3. 主要载流子是什么？

3. What carriers dominate?

ID-VG

负栅压增强为空穴分支，正栅压增强为电子分支；两侧都增强则是双极性传输。

Enhancement at negative gate bias indicates a hole branch; enhancement at positive gate bias indicates an electron branch; both branches indicate ambipolar transport.

4. 迁移率是否可被可信提取？

4. Is mobility extraction reliable?

gm

只有在栅控明确、接触影响可接受、迟滞不过大时，线性区迁移率才有较强解释力。

Linear-regime mobility is most meaningful when gate modulation is clear, contact effects are acceptable, and hysteresis is not dominant.

3. FET 测试与分析总流程

3. Full FET Testing and Analysis Workflow

正确顺序是先确认栅控和漏电，再看接触，再从转移曲线提取参数。

The correct order is to verify gate control and leakage first, then inspect contacts, and finally extract metrics from transfer curves.

器件与几何参数确认

Confirm device geometry

记录结构、W、L、tox、电极材料、薄膜处理和测试环境。没有这些，迁移率无法复现。

Record structure, W, L, tox, contact metal, film treatment, and testing environment. Without them, mobility cannot be reproduced.

测 IG-VG，排除栅漏电假象

Measure IG-VG to exclude leakage artifacts

IG 应远小于 ID，通常至少低 2-3 个数量级。若 IG 接近 ID，FET 结论不可靠。

IG should be far smaller than ID, typically by at least 2-3 orders of magnitude. If IG is comparable to ID, FET claims are unreliable.

测 ID-VD 输出曲线

Measure ID-VD output curves

固定不同 VG 扫 VD，用来判断栅控、欧姆/肖特基接触、接触受限和电流饱和情况。

Sweep VD at several fixed VG values to assess gate modulation, ohmic/Schottky contact behavior, contact limitation, and current saturation.

测 ID-VG 转移曲线

Measure ID-VG transfer curves

固定 VD 扫 VG。线性坐标用于斜率和迁移率，对数坐标用于开关比和关态电流。

Sweep VG at fixed VD. Linear scale is used for slope and mobility; log scale is used for on/off ratio and off-state current.

正扫/反扫比较

Compare forward and reverse sweeps

迟滞大通常说明陷阱态、水氧吸附、离子迁移或量子点表面态影响明显。

Large hysteresis often indicates traps, water/oxygen adsorption, ion migration, or strong CQD surface-state effects.

提取参数并建立物理归因

Extract metrics and assign physical origin

报告有效场效应迁移率、开关比、电流最小点、迟滞、接触性质和导电类型，不把接触/陷阱效应误写成本征迁移率。

Report effective field-effect mobility, on/off ratio, current minimum, hysteresis, contact behavior, and conduction type. Do not present contact/trap-limited values as intrinsic mobility.

4. 线性区迁移率公式推导

4. Linear-Regime Mobility Derivation

公式里的斜率必须来自固定 VD 下的 ID-VG 转移曲线，也就是跨导 gm。

The slope must come from the ID-VG transfer curve at fixed VD. It is the transconductance gm.

Step 1 电容 Step 1 Capacitance
Cox = ε0εrtox

背栅 Si/SiO₂/PbS 结构可近似看作平行板电容。300 nm SiO₂ 常用值为 Cox ≈ 1.15 × 10−8 F/cm2。

The back-gated Si/SiO₂/PbS stack can be approximated as a parallel-plate capacitor. For 300 nm SiO₂, Cox ≈ 1.15 × 10−8 F/cm2.

Step 2 电荷 Step 2 Charge
Q(x) = Cox [ VG − VT − V(x) ]

x 从源极指向漏极。源端附近 V(x)=0，漏端附近 V(x)=VD，因此沿沟道的有效栅压会逐渐变化。

The x-coordinate points from source to drain. Near the source V(x)=0; near the drain V(x)=VD, so the effective gate bias changes along the channel.

Step 3 漂移 Step 3 Drift
ID = WμQ(x)Ex = WμCox [VG−VT−V(x)] dVdx

沟道电流来自源漏电场驱动下的漂移输运。W 是沟道宽度，μ 是场效应迁移率。

The channel current is drift current driven by the source-drain electric field. W is the channel width and μ is the field-effect mobility.

Step 4 积分 Step 4 Integration
ID = μCox WL [(VG−VT)VD− VD22 ]

对 x: 0→L、V: 0→VD 积分，得到 FET 线性区/渐近沟道近似下的电流表达式。

Integrating over x: 0→L and V: 0→VD gives the current equation under the gradual-channel approximation.

Step 5 近似 Step 5 Approximation
When VD ≪ VG−VT, ID ≈ μCox WL (VG−VT)VD

若 VD 较小，可忽略 VD2/2 项。此时固定 VD，ID 与 VG 近似线性。

For small VD, the VD2/2 term can be neglected. At fixed VD, ID is approximately linear with VG.

Step 6 求导 Step 6 Derivative
gm = dIDdVG = μCox WL VD, μlin = LWCoxVD dIDdVG

最终斜率来自固定 VD 下的 ID-VG 转移曲线，不能使用 ID-VD 输出曲线斜率。

The final slope must be taken from the ID-VG transfer curve at fixed VD, not from the ID-VD output curve.

报告表达模板

Reporting template

在固定 VD = 0.28 V 下，取 ID-VG 转移曲线线性调控区域进行拟合，得到 gm = dID/dVG。根据 μlin = Lgm/(WCoxVD) 计算有效场效应迁移率。 At fixed VD = 0.28 V, the linear modulation region of the ID-VG transfer curve was fitted to obtain gm = dID/dVG. The effective field-effect mobility was calculated using μlin = Lgm/(WCoxVD).

适用边界

Applicability boundary

线性区公式默认沟道电流主要由栅压调控。如果接触势垒、陷阱充放电、离子迁移或迟滞占主导，应把结果表述为有效场效应迁移率，并同时报告测试条件与拟合区间。

The linear-regime formula assumes gate-controlled channel current. If contact barriers, trap charging, ion migration, or hysteresis dominate, report the result as effective field-effect mobility together with test conditions and fitting range.

5. 典型转移曲线结果示意

5. Typical Transfer-Curve Examples

判断导电类型时看哪一侧栅压使 |ID| 增大，而不是只看电流正负。

Conduction type is judged by which gate-bias side increases |ID|, not by the sign of current alone.

p-type

负栅压电流大

Higher current at negative VG

说明空穴更容易积累和传输，表现为空穴主导。

Hole accumulation and transport dominate.

n-type

正栅压电流大

Higher current at positive VG

说明电子更容易积累和传输，表现为电子主导。

Electron accumulation and transport dominate.

ambipolar

两侧都能导电

Both branches conduct

负栅压为空穴分支，正栅压为电子分支，中间是电流最小点。

Negative VG is the hole branch; positive VG is the electron branch; the middle point is the current minimum.

weak gate control

几乎不受栅压调控

Almost no gate modulation

可能薄膜太绝缘、太导电、接触限制、栅漏电或配体交换失败。

Possible causes include overly insulating/conductive film, contact limitation, gate leakage, or failed ligand exchange.

电流最小点偏移怎么解释？

How to interpret a shifted current minimum?

如果双极性转移曲线的电流最小点位于正栅压，例如 Vmin = +21.3 V，说明需要施加正栅压才能抵消零栅压附近已有的空穴，因此可描述为“零栅压附近具有 p 型掺杂倾向”。

If the current minimum of an ambipolar transfer curve appears at positive gate bias, e.g. Vmin = +21.3 V, positive gate bias is needed to compensate holes already present near zero gate bias. This indicates p-type doping tendency near VG = 0.

对 PbS CQD FET，通常使用“电流最小点”“电中性点”或“minimum-conductance point”来描述这个位置；这些表述更直接对应转移曲线中的可观测特征。

For PbS CQD FETs, this position is usually described as the "current minimum", "charge-neutrality point", or "minimum-conductance point", because these terms directly correspond to the observable feature in the transfer curve.

适合放进总结/论文的标准句

Standard wording for reports

PbS CQD FET 的输出曲线在不同栅压下呈现明显调制，说明沟道电导可被背栅有效控制。转移曲线显示器件在负/正栅压区域电流增强，分别对应空穴/电子积累。在线性区固定 VD 下，由 ID-VG 曲线的跨导 gm 提取有效场效应迁移率。 The output curves of the PbS CQD FET show clear modulation under different gate biases, indicating effective back-gate control of channel conductance. The transfer curve shows enhanced current under negative/positive gate bias, corresponding to hole/electron accumulation. In the linear regime, the effective field-effect mobility is extracted from the transconductance gm of the ID-VG curve at fixed VD.

结果阐释边界

Interpretation boundary

量子点薄膜 FET 的迁移率通常包含配体、量子点间耦合、陷阱态、接触注入和介质界面共同影响。因此报告时应说明这是器件条件下的有效迁移率，而不是单个 PbS 量子点或理想体材料的本征迁移率。

Mobility in CQD thin-film FETs is influenced by ligands, inter-dot coupling, traps, contact injection, and dielectric interfaces. It should therefore be reported as device-level effective mobility rather than the intrinsic mobility of a single PbS quantum dot or an ideal bulk material.
