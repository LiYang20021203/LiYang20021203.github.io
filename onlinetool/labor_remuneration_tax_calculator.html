Batch calculate withholding tax and net income from service remuneration

适用于中国居民个人取得劳务报酬时的按次或按月预扣预缴计算。输入多个税前劳务报酬金额，页面会逐条给出减除费用、应纳税所得额、适用预扣率、速算扣除数、应预扣税额和税后到手。

For China resident individuals receiving service remuneration, calculated on a per-payment or monthly withholding prepayment basis. Enter multiple gross amounts to see deductions, taxable income, rate, quick deduction, withholding tax, and net amount.

核心口径

Calculation Basis

  • 收入不超过 800 元：预扣税额为 0。
  • Income up to RMB 800: withholding tax is 0.
  • 800 元以上且不超过 4000 元：应纳税所得额 = 收入 - 800。
  • Above RMB 800 and up to RMB 4,000: taxable income = income - 800.
  • 超过 4000 元：应纳税所得额 = 收入 × 80%。
  • Above RMB 4,000: taxable income = income x 80%.
  • 应预扣税额 = 应纳税所得额 × 预扣率 - 速算扣除数。
  • Tax withheld = taxable income x withholding rate - quick deduction.
