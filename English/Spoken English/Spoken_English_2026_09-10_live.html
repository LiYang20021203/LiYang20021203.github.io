Choice, Inequality & Emotional Support

We discussed how personal choices can be shaped by poverty, gender inequality, and unequal power, then moved into a relationship question: how to acknowledge someone’s pain without dismissing it, becoming overwhelmed by it, or immediately trying to solve it.

01 · Social structure & choice

I don’t think surrogacy can be discussed only as an individual choice, because individual choices don’t happen in a vacuum.
中文
我不认为代孕只能被当作个人选择来讨论，因为个人选择并不是在真空中发生的。
Phrase breakdown
individual choice
CORE›
个人选择。讨论社会议题时很常用。
don’t happen in a vacuum
CORE›
并不是脱离背景独立发生的。用于强调选择受到社会、经济和文化环境影响。
People’s decisions are shaped by their social circumstances, including the wealth gap, gender inequality, and economic pressure.
中文
人的决定会受到其社会处境影响，包括贫富差距、性别不平等以及经济压力。
Phrase breakdown
social circumstances
USEFUL›
社会处境 / 社会环境。
wealth gap
CORE›
贫富差距；也可以说 the rich-poor divide。
gender inequality
/ˈdʒendər ˌɪnɪˈkwɑːləti/
CORE›
性别不平等。
economic pressure
USEFUL›
经济压力。
A serious power imbalance can make economically vulnerable women easier to exploit, which is why some people describe commercial surrogacy as a form of exploitation.
中文
严重的权力不对等可能会让经济上处于弱势的女性更容易受到剥削，因此一些人把商业代孕描述为一种剥削形式。
Phrase breakdown
power imbalance
CORE›
权力不对等。
economically vulnerable women
vulnerable /ˈvʌln(ə)rəbəl/
USEFUL›
经济上处于弱势的女性。比反复使用 poor women 更准确。
exploit someone
EXPAND›
剥削 / 利用某人。
exploitation
/ˌeksplɔɪˈteɪʃən/
EXPAND›
剥削；名词。
At the same time, talking about structural inequality does not mean denying women’s agency, autonomy, or individual responsibility.
中文
与此同时，讨论结构性不平等并不意味着否认女性的主体性、自主权或个人责任。
Phrase breakdown
structural inequality
CORE›
结构性不平等。
agency
EXPAND›
主体性；自主作出选择并采取行动的能力。
autonomy
/ɔːˈtɑːnəmi/
EXPAND›
自主权。常见搭配：individual autonomy, reproductive autonomy。
individual responsibility
CORE›
个人责任。
We need to look at the bigger picture instead of reducing a structural issue to personal morality or oversimplifying the issue.
中文
我们需要看整个问题，而不是把结构性问题简单归结为个人道德，或者把问题过度简化。
Phrase breakdown
look at the bigger picture
CORE›
看整个问题 / 看大局。
reduce A to B
EXPAND›
把复杂的 A 简化或归结为 B。
a structural issue
CORE›
结构性问题。
oversimplify the issue
EXPAND›
过度简化问题。

02 · Discussing different viewpoints

I see your point, and I get where you’re coming from, but from my perspective there is another side to the issue.
中文
我理解你的观点，也理解你为什么这么想，但从我的角度来看，这个问题还有另一面。
Phrase breakdown
I see your point.
CORE›
我理解你的观点。并不等于 I agree with you。
I get where you’re coming from.
CORE›
我理解你为什么会这么想 / 我理解你的出发点。
from my perspective
USEFUL›
从我的角度来看。
Individual responsibility matters; at the same time, social inequality can play a role in the choices people realistically have.
中文
个人责任很重要；与此同时，社会不平等也会影响一个人现实中能够拥有的选择。
Phrase breakdown
individual responsibility matters
CORE›
个人责任很重要。
at the same time
USEFUL›
同时 / 另一方面。口语里很自然。
play a role
USEFUL›
发挥作用 / 是其中一个因素。
That being said, it isn’t black and white, and I think we need a more nuanced view.
中文
话虽如此，这个问题并不是非黑即白的，我认为我们需要一个更有层次的看法。
Phrase breakdown
That being said, …
USEFUL›
话虽如此 / 尽管如此。
It isn’t black and white.
CORE›
这不是非黑即白的问题。
a more nuanced view
EXPAND›
更有层次、更细致的看法。
Poverty can contribute to the problem without being the only cause, so I don’t want to oversimplify it.
中文
贫困可能会促成这个问题，但并不是唯一原因，所以我不想把它过度简化。
Phrase breakdown
contribute to
USEFUL›
促成 / 导致。比 cause 更谨慎。
the only cause
USEFUL›
唯一原因。
oversimplify it
EXPAND›
把它过度简化。
When someone says, “She chose it herself,” I want to say, “It’s more complicated than that.”
中文
当有人说“这是她自己选的”时，我会想说：“事情比这复杂得多。”
Phrase breakdown
It’s more complicated than that.
CORE›
事情没那么简单 / 比那更复杂。

03 · Emotional support & difficult feelings

She’s been through a lot, and I don’t want to dismiss or invalidate her feelings.
中文
她经历了很多，我不想忽视她的感受，也不想让她觉得自己的感受是不合理的。
Phrase breakdown
She’s been through a lot.
CORE›
她经历了很多。很自然、很高频。
dismiss someone’s feelings
USEFUL›
不把某人的感受当回事。
invalidate someone’s feelings
USEFUL›
让对方觉得自己的感受不合理、不应该存在。
When she talks about something painful, my first instinct is to fix the problem or make the pain go away.
中文
当她讲起痛苦的事情时，我的第一反应就是解决问题，或者让这种痛苦消失。
Phrase breakdown
my first instinct is to…
CORE›
我的第一反应是……。非常高频。
fix the problem
USEFUL›
解决问题。
make the pain go away
USEFUL›
让痛苦消失。
But sometimes she needs emotional support rather than advice, and she may just need me to listen.
中文
但有时候她需要的是情绪支持，而不是建议；她可能只是需要我听她说。
Phrase breakdown
emotional support rather than advice
CORE›
情绪支持，而不是建议。
just listen
CORE›
只是倾听。关系沟通中非常常用。
It’s not that I don’t care; I sometimes feel emotionally overwhelmed or emotionally drained after difficult conversations.
中文
并不是我不在乎；有时候困难的谈话会让我在情绪上难以承受，甚至觉得被耗空。
Phrase breakdown
It’s not that I don’t care.
CORE›
并不是我不在乎。可复用框架：It’s not that…, I just…
emotionally overwhelmed
USEFUL›
情绪上被压得难以承受。
emotionally drained
USEFUL›
情绪被耗空。
I need to acknowledge her pain, sit with uncomfortable feelings, and stay with the discomfort instead of trying to escape it.
中文
我需要学会正视她的痛苦，与不舒服的情绪共处，并在不适感里停留，而不是马上逃避。
Phrase breakdown
acknowledge someone’s pain
acknowledge /əkˈnɑːlɪdʒ/
CORE›
承认、正视某人的痛苦。
sit with uncomfortable feelings
CORE›
允许不舒服的情绪存在，不立即消除它。
stay with the discomfort
USEFUL›
在不适感存在时先停留，不急着逃跑。
escape from pain
USEFUL›
逃避痛苦。
I can ask, “Do you want me to just listen, or do you want to talk about what we can actually do?”
中文
我可以问：“你希望我只是听你说，还是想一起讨论我们实际上能做什么？”
Phrase breakdown
Do you want me to just listen, or do you want to talk about what we can actually do?
CORE›
先确认对方此刻需要倾听，还是需要一起解决问题。
Learning to deal with pain is different from trying to eliminate it; sometimes we have to cope with it or even learn to live with it.
中文
学会面对痛苦不同于试图把它消灭掉；有时候我们需要应对它，甚至学会与它共存。
Phrase breakdown
deal with pain
CORE›
面对 / 处理痛苦。
cope with pain
USEFUL›
应对痛苦。
live with pain
USEFUL›
与长期存在的痛苦共存。

PLS Love: Chemistry, Tension & Push–Pull

We practiced telling a romance story in natural spoken English: two women are drawn to each other despite class differences, prejudice, hurt, pride, and reasons to stay apart. The most useful language described attraction, emotional tension, separation, reunion, and the conflict between what they say and what they still feel.

01 · Storytelling & character background

Pan and Phakinee come from very different social backgrounds: Phakinee comes from a wealthy family and has a high social status, while Pan works as a hostess at an upscale club.
中文
Pan 和 Phakinee 来自非常不同的社会背景：Phakinee 来自富裕家庭，社会地位很高，而 Pan 在高档会所做 hostess。
Phrase breakdown
class difference / status difference
USEFUL›
阶级差异 / 社会地位差异。
come from a wealthy family
USEFUL›
来自富裕家庭。
have a high social status
USEFUL›
社会地位较高。
a hostess at an upscale club
CORE›
高档夜店 / 会所的女招待、陪酒女。
Pan has clear boundaries, but Phakinee initially looks down on her because of prejudice and because she doesn’t know what Pan has been through.
中文
Pan 有明确的个人界限，但一开始 Phakinee 因为偏见而看不起她，而且她并不知道 Pan 经历过什么。
Phrase breakdown
have clear boundaries
CORE›
有明确的界限 / 底线。
look down on someone
CORE›
看不起某人。
prejudice
/ˈpredʒədɪs/
EXPAND›
偏见。
what someone has been through
CORE›
某人曾经经历过什么。
After Pan leaves, Phakinee wakes up alone and feels abandoned and betrayed.
中文
Pan 离开后，Phakinee 醒来发现自己一个人，并感到被抛弃和背叛。
Phrase breakdown
wake up alone
USEFUL›
醒来时发现自己一个人。
feel abandoned and betrayed
abandoned /əˈbændənd/
USEFUL›
感到被抛弃、被背叛。
Years later, they meet again and are reunited, but neither of them has fully let go.
中文
多年以后，她们再次见面并重逢，但两个人都没有真正放下过去。
Phrase breakdown
meet again years later
USEFUL›
多年后再次见面。
be reunited
USEFUL›
重逢。
can’t let go / haven’t let go
CORE›
无法放下 / 还没有放下。
That setup gives the story a strong enemies-to-lovers dynamic and a lot of push-and-pull tension.
中文
这样的设定让故事拥有很强的 enemies-to-lovers 关系模式，也充满感情拉扯。
Phrase breakdown
enemies-to-lovers
CORE›
从敌对或冲突关系逐渐发展为恋人的故事类型。
push-and-pull tension
CORE›
靠近、退开、再被吸引回来的感情拉扯。
